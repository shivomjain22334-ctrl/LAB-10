### LAB 10 – Image Generation

In Lab 10, I learned how to create and refine aviation-related visual assets using a **Five-Part Structured Image Prompt**. The five components were Subject, Medium/Visual Style, Environment, Lighting, and Aspect Ratio with constraints.

The activity involved generating a professional photorealistic image of young travellers inside a modern international airport terminal. The initial image had problems such as harsh lighting, unwanted pseudo-text, and cluttered subject placement. Instead of changing the entire prompt, a single variable was modified: the lighting was changed to warm golden-hour sunset light passing through floor-to-ceiling glass windows.

The revised image showed improved lighting, better depth, cleaner composition, and fewer unwanted visual artifacts. Negative constraints such as **no text, signage letters, or commercial airline logos** were also maintained. This lab taught me that structured image prompts and controlled single-variable changes can significantly improve AI-generated visual content for professional aviation campaigns.
